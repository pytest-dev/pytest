Failed subtests are no longer double-counted in the terminal summary line: a failing subtest used to be reported as a regular test failure *in addition to* its enclosing test being failed for containing a failed subtest, so a single failed subtest inflated the failure count by one. Failed subtests are now tallied separately as ``subtests failed``, while their tracebacks and short test summary entries remain in the ``=== FAILURES ===`` section.
