The contribution documentation was reorganised: setup instructions now lead with ``uv``,
the process the team follows after a contribution arrives moved into a separate maintenance
guide, and the AI/LLM policy separates its requirements from its rationale.

The AI/LLM policy is also stricter: disclosing AI use is now mandatory, for maintainers too,
and hiding it gets the account banned.

A new :file:`AGENTS.md` gives coding agents the project's setup, layout and conventions,
along with the instruction to post nothing that a supervising human has not read and approved.
