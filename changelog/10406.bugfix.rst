:ref:`filterwarnings <pytest.mark.filterwarnings ref>` marks on a closer level now take precedence
over marks on a farther one: a mark from parametrization over one on the test function, the function
over its class, and a class over the module. Previously the farthest mark won, so for example a
module or class level ``filterwarnings("error")`` overrode a test's own ``filterwarnings("ignore")``.
